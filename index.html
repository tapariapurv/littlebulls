Paper trading for kids

Paper trading means practising buying and selling stocks with pretend money. The prices are real, but nothing you win or lose is. It is the safest way for a child to find out how the stock market works, because a mistake costs nothing and still teaches something.

LittleBulls is a free paper trading app made for kids. You pick how much pretend money to start with, search for a company you know, and make your first trade. Bully the bull explains the tricky words in plain language along the way.

What kids can do on LittleBulls

  • Trade real stocks. Pick from 41 exchanges around the world, like NSE, NASDAQ, NYSE and London, at real market prices.
  • Read real charts. Candles and lines, from today back to five years ago.
  • Place different kinds of orders. Buy right now, at a chosen price, or when a price is reached, even while the market is closed.
  • Try different ideas. Keep several portfolios and see which one does better.
  • See wins and losses clearly. What is locked in, what is still open, and how much is free to use.
  • Research companies. Compare two stocks, read news about them, and see what 1,000 from a year ago would be worth today.

Questions parents ask

Is any real money involved?

No. There are no payments and no brokerage account. All the money in LittleBulls is pretend, so nobody can lose real money.

How much does it cost?

LittleBulls is free.

Does it give stock tips?

No. It is a place to practise and learn. What happened to a stock in the past cannot promise what it will do next.

What does a child need to start?

A nickname and an email address, or a Google account. We suggest a parent helps set it up.
